# Monday 2026-08-17 · Discipline

> "If you can only execute when you feel like executing, you will lose 100 times out of 100."

Monday is when feeling-based people fall behind. They wait for energy that won't come, blame the timing, and watch the disciplined version of themselves outpace them all week. You're not waiting on a feeling today.

## The method

> "Discipline is the ability to give yourself a command, and then follow it."

Make the command precise: not 'work hard today' but 'work on X for 90 minutes starting at 9.' Vague commands get vague follow-through. Specific commands get executed because there's nothing left to negotiate.

## The mindset

> "A clean start is not found. It is forced."

Your mind respects what you repeat. If you repeat negotiation, it will bring better excuses. If you repeat execution, it eventually stops asking for permission.

## Today's call

Complete one preventive action in the first half of the day. Do not chase drama until the obvious discipline is done.

Mood follows action. The reverse is fiction.
