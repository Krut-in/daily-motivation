# Tuesday 2026-10-20 · Focus

> "The quality of your output is tied to the discipline of your inputs."

Tuesday can become an input buffet. News, messages, dashboards, feeds, opinions, half-useful updates. Some of it is information. Much of it is just friction wearing a useful mask.

## The method

> "The best way to silence them is through activity. Through results."

Focus isn't a feeling. It's an exclusion habit. Decide right now what you will not work on this week, and let that exclusion be the moat around your one thing.

## The mindset

> "If you have scattered focus, you'll have a scattered life."

Your attention is the highest-value asset you spend each day. Most of it gets spent reactively, on whatever pings loudest. The disciplined version of you decides where it goes before anyone else has a chance to ask.

## Today's call

Name the main thing in one sentence and give it the best 75 minutes you have left today. Protect the block like it belongs to someone important.

Cleaner inputs create stronger work.
