# Sunday 2026-10-11 · Resilience

> "When the defining moment comes, either you define it or it defines you."

Sunday is the day this week's defining moments either become yours or own you. Most people let them happen and then build a story around the happening. The disciplined move is to choose your definition first, and let the moments fall into the frame you decided.

## The method

> "The moment of extreme adversity is the best moment for audacity."

When everything in your week says 'be smaller,' that's exactly when to be larger. The dose-response curve on courage inverts under pressure. The audacious move you delayed is the one Sunday is asking you to take.

## The mindset

> "Do not waste the bruise by refusing the lesson."

Resilience is not being untouched. It is becoming less fragile after contact. The lesson matters because it changes the next collision.

## Today's call

Spend twenty minutes clearing one source of friction for tomorrow. Lay out the workout, write the first task, clean the desk, or decide what gets ignored until noon.

What you call this week is who you become next week.
