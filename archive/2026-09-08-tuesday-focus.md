# Tuesday 2026-09-08 · Focus

> "You got to pass up on good to get to great."

Tuesday is when 'good' opportunities start crowding out the 'great' one. Every meeting that wasn't on Sunday's plan and every helpful favor for someone else looks innocent on its own. Together they crowd out the work that's actually yours.

## The method

> "Every open option taxes the work that actually matters."

Focus is not adding intensity. It is subtracting leakage. Name the one thing that matters most today, then name the two things that are most likely to impersonate urgency and steal from it.

## The mindset

> "The next level needs less noise than the current one tolerates."

Your attention is not weak because distraction is available. It is weakened by repeated permission. Today, remove the permission and see how much stronger the work becomes.

## Today's call

Block 90 minutes of focused work this morning. Phone in another room. No tabs that aren't the work. When the discomfort hits and you reach for a distraction, just notice the reach and return.

A garden is just a forest someone said no to.
