# Sunday 2026-08-09 · Resilience

> "When the defining moment comes, either you define it or it defines you."

Sunday is the day this week's defining moments either become yours or own you. Most people let them happen and then build a story around the happening. The disciplined move is to choose your definition first, and let the moments fall into the frame you decided.

## The method

> "If you do what is easy, your life will be hard. If you do what is hard, your life will be easy."

Resilience isn't about being unbreakable. It's about choosing the harder thing on the day you most want the easier. Today the easy thing is to stay in bed and wait for Monday energy. The harder thing is to run while it's raining.

## The mindset

> "Resilience is what happens after the emotional reaction passes."

Resilience is not just taking the hit. It is refusing to take the same hit the same way forever. The adjustment is where growth becomes visible.

## Today's call

Pick the one thing this Sunday that's been quietly grinding on you. Maybe a decision you've been deferring, or a project you've been letting drift. Pick one and address it before bed.

What you call this week is who you become next week.
