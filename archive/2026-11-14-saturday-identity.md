# Saturday 2026-11-14 · Identity

> "You become the leader of your space because you're not following anybody. You're innovating a unique path of your own."

Saturday is when comparison gets loud. The week's social feed has fed you a hundred metrics about other people's lives. Today is the day to put all of that down and remember the one thing that's actually yours: your specific path, which only you can walk.

## The method

> "What makes a lion the king of the jungle is its mentality."

A lion isn't born a lion. It earns the title through the hunt, not the kill. Every Saturday you commit to the unglamorous parts of your craft is a day the lion in you stops being theoretical.

## The mindset

> "If no one thinks you're crazy, you're not yet operating to the outer limits of your potential."

The version of you that scares the comfortable people around you is usually the right one. If your work makes someone uncomfortable enough to call you 'too intense,' that's a signal. Don't soften it. Refine it.

## Today's call

Set one Saturday standard and meet it before the day spreads out. Keep it simple enough that there is no excuse to delay.

The path no one else is walking is the only one that becomes yours.
