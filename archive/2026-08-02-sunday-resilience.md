# Sunday 2026-08-02 · Resilience

> "There's no game without pain. The person who refuses to be denied comes back, again and again."

Sunday is the day you decide whether last week's pain was a wall or a doorway. Same pain, different read. The version of you that calls it a wall stops here. The version that calls it a doorway shows up Monday with more capacity than they had last Sunday.

## The method

> "The reset is part of the fight."

Review the week without drama. What drained you? What strengthened you? What needs to be changed before Monday? Resilience gets practical when the answer becomes a calendar block or a boundary.

## The mindset

> "Your character is put to the test when your back is against the wall."

Backed against the wall is just data. It tells you what you're made of, and it tells you what you didn't know you could do. Sunday is the day to face the wall calmly and discover both.

## Today's call

Write one adjustment for the coming week and make it concrete: time, place, trigger, or boundary. Then do something that restores your body without numbing your mind.

Pain is data. Refusal to read it is the only loss.
