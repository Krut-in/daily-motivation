# Sunday 2026-10-04 · Resilience

> "There's no game without pain. The person who refuses to be denied comes back, again and again."

Sunday is the day you decide whether last week's pain was a wall or a doorway. Same pain, different read. The version of you that calls it a wall stops here. The version that calls it a doorway shows up Monday with more capacity than they had last Sunday.

## The method

> "Resilience turns pain into preparation."

Look at the one place you got hit hardest this week. Ask what would have made you sturdier. Then set that thing up before Monday starts asking for it.

## The mindset

> "Two primary choices in life: accept conditions as they exist, or take responsibility to change them."

By the end of Sunday, you've made one of these two choices about this week. Acceptance and responsibility aren't the same as resignation and effort. The disciplined version of you accepts what happened and takes responsibility for what happens next.

## Today's call

Pick the one thing this Sunday that the broken version of you would dismiss as too much. Maybe a long conversation, or a project you'd been avoiding all week. Begin it small. Just begin.

Pain is data. Refusal to read it is the only loss.
