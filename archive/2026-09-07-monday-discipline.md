# Monday 2026-09-07 · Discipline

> "I set the tone for my day. Nobody's going to mess with my day."

Monday's first hour is the most important hour of the week. Whoever or whatever sets the tone of that hour usually sets the tone of the whole week. Today, you set it before anyone or anything else gets a chance.

## The method

> "Your ego is digging a grave. A grave of comfort you'll voluntarily crawl into."

Comfort feels like rest. It's actually slow surrender. Every time you postpone a small discipline, you're not 'taking it easy,' you're voting against the version of you that wanted something different last week.

## The mindset

> "Your mind will always choose the path of least resistance. Train it to choose the path of most resistance instead."

Discipline is the choice your mind makes after you've trained it to choose against itself. It feels alien at first because your default neural circuit is set to ease. Every act of friction is rewiring.

## Today's call

Complete one preventive action in the first half of the day. Do not chase drama until the obvious discipline is done.

How you start Monday determines what Friday looks like.
