# Thursday 2026-08-13 · Belief

> "Do not let one tired emotion overrule a week of evidence."

Thursday emotion can be persuasive because it has fatigue on its side. It will tell you the work is not working. Before you believe it, look for evidence. What did you finish? What did you resist? What promise did you keep?

## The method

> "Your mind follows the evidence you keep feeding it."

Belief is trained by selecting better evidence. Find one kept promise from this week, even a small one, and let that be the proof you work from today instead of the miss you keep replaying.

## The mindset

> "Use the pain as fuel to go harder."

Thursday's tiredness is information about your current capacity, but it isn't a verdict about your potential. The disciplined move is to use the tiredness as the input that makes today's effort meaningful, instead of the excuse that ends it.

## Today's call

Pick the one task today that the tired version of you would skip. Do it before you're allowed to call it a day. Not for the result. For the proof.

Receipts beat moods.
