# Tuesday 2026-08-25 · Focus

> "The quality of your output is tied to the discipline of your inputs."

Tuesday can become an input buffet. News, messages, dashboards, feeds, opinions, half-useful updates. Some of it is information. Much of it is just friction wearing a useful mask.

## The method

> "Focus is what happens when you stop feeding every appetite for novelty."

Choose a cleaner input environment for one work block. Put only the materials required for the task in front of you. Let the absence of noise do some of the work.

## The mindset

> "Cut off anything that doesn't support you. Embed the long-term goal in your mind."

The hardest part of focus is grieving what you're saying no to. Every yes to your one thing is a small no to something else you also wanted. The discipline is letting that grief be the price of becoming who you said you'd be.

## Today's call

Block 90 minutes of focused work this morning. Phone in another room. No tabs that aren't the work. When the discomfort hits and you reach for a distraction, just notice the reach and return.

Cleaner inputs create stronger work.
