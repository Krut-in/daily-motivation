# Monday 2026-09-28 · Discipline

> "The disciplined move is usually smaller and earlier than the dramatic one."

Monday drama is optional more often than it feels. A lot of chaos is just delayed discipline with better lighting. Today, do the boring thing early before it becomes the emergency thing later.

## The method

> "Excuses sound best to the person who is making them up."

Notice what excuse comes up first this morning, and write it down. Just the words. Tomorrow, when the same excuse arrives, you'll see it for what it is. A regular companion, not a true reason.

## The mindset

> "Without discipline, you're not prepared for the future."

Today's discipline isn't about today. It's about the future version of you who'll inherit whatever shape today leaves them in. Every undisciplined Monday is a small theft from the person you'll be in six months.

## Today's call

Before opening the loudest inbox, complete one action that makes the rest of the week easier. Keep it measurable.

Early discipline is cheaper than late panic.
