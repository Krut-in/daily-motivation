# Monday 2026-08-31 · Discipline

> "You give yourself a pass. You know you should be further in life, but you keep letting you slide."

Monday is when the most generous person in your life shows up to give you a free pass: yourself. You're more lenient with you than anyone else has ever been. Today is the day to be the strict version of yourself you'd never tolerate from anyone else.

## The method

> "Do not wait until the day is on fire to become serious."

Find the task that prevents three future problems. It might be planning, paying, cleaning, training, writing, or sending the message. Handle it before the day has enough noise to make delay sound reasonable.

## The mindset

> "Without discipline, you're not prepared for the future."

Today's discipline isn't about today. It's about the future version of you who'll inherit whatever shape today leaves them in. Every undisciplined Monday is a small theft from the person you'll be in six months.

## Today's call

Pick the one Monday discipline you'd be tempted to skip. Maybe a cold shower, or 90 focused minutes before email. Run it before any negotiation begins.

Discipline is just self-respect with a schedule.
