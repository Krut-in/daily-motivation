# Monday 2026-10-05 · Discipline

> "If you can only execute when you feel like executing, you will lose 100 times out of 100."

Monday is when feeling-based people fall behind. They wait for energy that won't come, blame the timing, and watch the disciplined version of themselves outpace them all week. You're not waiting on a feeling today.

## The method

> "It's high time that discipline became your best friend. Your daily companion."

Treat discipline less like a punishment and more like a relationship. The disciplined person doesn't fight themselves, they walk with themselves. The morning command isn't a threat. It's an agreement.

## The mindset

> "Order is built before urgency arrives."

Discipline is not intensity. Intensity spikes and disappears. Discipline is a quiet prepayment against the version of life where everything costs more because you waited.

## Today's call

Pick one specific command for today, with a clear action and a start time. Write it down. Execute it. Don't negotiate.

Mood follows action. The reverse is fiction.
