# Saturday 2026-08-29 · Identity

> "Do something that sucks every single day of your life. That's how you grow."

Saturday is when most people lower the bar because no one's grading. The whole week was a performance for someone external. Today no one's watching, so today reveals who you actually are when there's no scoreboard.

## The method

> "Get over your feelings. Feelings go up and down."

The mistake is treating your feelings as data about whether to act. They aren't. They're weather. The disciplined version of you acts according to the schedule, and the feelings come along once the action begins.

## The mindset

> "Commitment begins after the audience disappears."

You do not become disciplined in public. Public only reveals what private has already built. The version of you that wins later is being negotiated right now in quiet hours.

## Today's call

Choose one promise from the week that still matters. Close the loop today, even if the loop is small.

Identity is what survives the absence of an audience.
