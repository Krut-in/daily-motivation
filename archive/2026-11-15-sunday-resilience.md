# Sunday 2026-11-15 · Resilience

> "Tough times have not come to stay. They have come to pass."

Sunday is the day the week's accumulated weight catches up. Whatever happened Mon-Fri left a residue on you, and the temptation now is to read that residue as a verdict. It isn't. It's weather.

## The method

> "The choice is yours of what it becomes."

Last week's events are fixed. Your interpretation isn't. Today, take the worst thing that happened this week and write down the most resourceful possible reading of it. Not optimistic. Resourceful. What does this hand you that you didn't have before?

## The mindset

> "Resilience is what happens after the emotional reaction passes."

Resilience is not just taking the hit. It is refusing to take the same hit the same way forever. The adjustment is where growth becomes visible.

## Today's call

Pick one thing this Sunday that the broken version of you would skip. Maybe a long walk in the cold, or finishing the project you abandoned Wednesday. Do it for one reason: doing the thing teaches your nervous system who's actually in charge, regardless of mood.

Tough people last longer than tough times.
