# Sunday 2026-10-18 · Resilience

> "Rest is useful when it restores the fighter, not when it hides the fight."

Sunday can become a soft exit from responsibility if you let it. The body wants rest and the mind wants escape. Give the body what it needs, but do not let escape dress itself up as recovery.

## The method

> "The choice is yours of what it becomes."

Last week's events are fixed. Your interpretation isn't. Today, take the worst thing that happened this week and write down the most resourceful possible reading of it. Not optimistic. Resourceful. What does this hand you that you didn't have before?

## The mindset

> "Your character is put to the test when your back is against the wall."

Backed against the wall is just data. It tells you what you're made of, and it tells you what you didn't know you could do. Sunday is the day to face the wall calmly and discover both.

## Today's call

Pick the one thing this Sunday that the broken version of you would dismiss as too much. Maybe a long conversation, or a project you'd been avoiding all week. Begin it small. Just begin.

Recover like someone who intends to return sharper.
