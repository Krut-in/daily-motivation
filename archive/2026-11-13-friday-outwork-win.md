# Friday 2026-11-13 · Outwork / Win

> "Your survival rate for every last bad day is 100%. You're batting a thousand."

Friday closes the week, and the brain wants to remind you of every hard moment. Every email that landed wrong and every plan that didn't go. But the data underneath is the same as every prior week: you survived all of them. Every single one.

## The method

> "I will not be denied. This will not be it for my life."

Outworking everyone isn't about hours. It's about declaring something publicly to yourself: this is not the version I'm settling for. Make that declaration today, then take one specific action that proves you meant it.

## The mindset

> "If you don't have any critics, you're probably not doing anything great."

A Friday with critics is a Friday where you're visible. Visibility means you're moving. The version of you that no one criticizes is also the version that disappears.

## Today's call

Close one loop before the week ends. If it cannot be finished, define the next action so clearly that it stops taking up background space.

What you finish on Friday is what makes Monday possible.
