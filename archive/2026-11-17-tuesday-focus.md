# Tuesday 2026-11-17 · Focus

> "Where focus goes, energy flows. Clarity equals power."

Tuesday is the day clarity wins. The blurry version of your week with vague goals and half-defined priorities gets devoured by everyone else's clear ones. Today, get specific about the single outcome that has to happen by Friday.

## The method

> "Get rid of the debate. Make the binary code decision: yes or no."

Most lost focus is just unmade decisions sitting in your head. Should I do this? Should I check that? Each unmade decision drains the battery. Convert each one into a yes or no, and the battery refills.

## The mindset

> "Cut off anything that doesn't support you. Embed the long-term goal in your mind."

The hardest part of focus is grieving what you're saying no to. Every yes to your one thing is a small no to something else you also wanted. The discipline is letting that grief be the price of becoming who you said you'd be.

## Today's call

Run one output-first block before consuming optional inputs. Make something before you check everything.

Clarity is just the willingness to choose.
