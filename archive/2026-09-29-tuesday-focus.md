# Tuesday 2026-09-29 · Focus

> "Focus is the courage to close doors that still look useful."

Tuesday is where good options start pretending to be obligations. A call here, a side task there, a little harmless checking. None of it looks fatal alone. Together it steals the depth the real work needs.

## The method

> "Only those who risk going too far can find out how far one can go."

Focus is partly a permission to push past where reasonable people stop. Reasonable is the enemy. The Tuesday version of you that's reasonable is the one who'll never know what the unreasonable version could have built.

## The mindset

> "A thoroughbred wears blinders so it can stay in its lane and see only the finish line."

Your peripheral vision is the enemy of focus. You don't need to see what others are doing. You only need to see your finish line and what to do in the next 90 minutes to move toward it.

## Today's call

Identify your single most important task today. Give it 90 minutes before lunch with everything else closed. The other things will still be there at 2 PM.

A focused day is a protected day.
