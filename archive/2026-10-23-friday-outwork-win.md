# Friday 2026-10-23 · Outwork / Win

> "The price you pay for chasing the dream is far smaller than the price you'll pay if you never do."

Friday closes the work week, and the temptation is to coast. The version of you that wants to coast is the version paying the bigger price later. The harder Friday is the one you'll never regret on Sunday night.

## The method

> "You just begin. You solve one problem, then you solve the next."

Outworking the week isn't about heroic Friday energy. It's about the smallest unit of progress. Solve the next problem, not the whole pile. Friday is the day to remember that the work is just one solved problem at a time.

## The mindset

> "Win the finish, not the fantasy."

This is not perfectionism. Perfectionism hides from shipping. Standards improve what is already moving. Keep it moving and make it cleaner.

## Today's call

Pick one task today where you're the only one who'd notice if you dropped the standard. Hold it at the higher standard anyway. The Friday version of you who doesn't compromise is the one who deserves the weekend.

What costs you everything once costs you nothing forever.
