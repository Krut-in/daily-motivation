# Monday 2026-11-09 · Discipline

> "A soft Monday becomes a heavy week."

Monday does not need a warmup lap. It needs a signal. If the first signal is drift, the whole day learns drift. If the first signal is execution, the day has to organize around that.

## The method

> "Do not wait until the day is on fire to become serious."

Find the task that prevents three future problems. It might be planning, paying, cleaning, training, writing, or sending the message. Handle it before the day has enough noise to make delay sound reasonable.

## The mindset

> "You have to learn to do when you don't want to do."

Discipline isn't about willpower. It's about removing the negotiation between you and the action. The disciplined person doesn't argue with themselves about whether to start. They just start, and the body follows.

## Today's call

Pick one non-negotiable action and do it in the first hour you control. Do not improve the plan first. Execute the opening rep.

Set the standard before the day sets one for you.
