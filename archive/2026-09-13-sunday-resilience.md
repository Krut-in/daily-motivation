# Sunday 2026-09-13 · Resilience

> "Tough times have not come to stay. They have come to pass."

Sunday is the day the week's accumulated weight catches up. Whatever happened Mon-Fri left a residue on you, and the temptation now is to read that residue as a verdict. It isn't. It's weather.

## The method

> "Quitting guarantees the failure."

There's a quiet kind of quitting that doesn't look like quitting. It's the slow withdrawal of effort, dressed up as 'this wasn't really for me anyway.' Today, name the thing you've been quietly quitting, and decide whether to actually quit it or actually try.

## The mindset

> "Two primary choices in life: accept conditions as they exist, or take responsibility to change them."

By the end of Sunday, you've made one of these two choices about this week. Acceptance and responsibility aren't the same as resignation and effort. The disciplined version of you accepts what happened and takes responsibility for what happens next.

## Today's call

Write down one recurring pressure and one structural fix. Put the fix somewhere Monday cannot miss it.

Tough people last longer than tough times.
