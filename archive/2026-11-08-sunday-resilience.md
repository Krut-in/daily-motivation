# Sunday 2026-11-08 · Resilience

> "A hard lesson only helps if you carry it into the next decision."

Sunday is not only for recovery. It is for translation. The week taught you something through friction. If you do not translate it into a changed behavior, the same lesson returns louder.

## The method

> "If you do what is easy, your life will be hard. If you do what is hard, your life will be easy."

Resilience isn't about being unbreakable. It's about choosing the harder thing on the day you most want the easier. Today the easy thing is to stay in bed and wait for Monday energy. The harder thing is to run while it's raining.

## The mindset

> "Hold on to faith. Things will get better. Those experiences shape you into who you need to be."

Resilience isn't about not getting hit. It's about being a different shape after the hit. Sunday is the day you take inventory of the new shape, even when you're sore from the change.

## Today's call

Spend twenty minutes clearing one source of friction for tomorrow. Lay out the workout, write the first task, clean the desk, or decide what gets ignored until noon.

The lesson is not complete until behavior changes.
