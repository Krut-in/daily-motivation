# Sunday 2026-09-20 · Resilience

> "It is staying with the breaking that produces the blessing."

Sunday is when the week's accumulated weight asks you to quit. The week broke you in small ways you barely noticed at the time. Today's only job is to stay with the breaking long enough for the mark it leaves to be worth carrying.

## The method

> "Putting the next foot in front of the next foot at least keeps you in the game."

Resilience this Sunday isn't about epic comeback energy. It's about the next foot. The version of you that just keeps walking, even slowly, eventually arrives. The version that stops to wait for inspiration usually doesn't.

## The mindset

> "A hard week can either make you smaller or make your system smarter."

You are allowed to be tired without becoming careless. You are allowed to reset without retreating. The fighter who lasts is the one who learns how to recover on purpose.

## Today's call

Pick the one thing this Sunday that the broken version of you would dismiss as too much. Maybe a long conversation, or a project you'd been avoiding all week. Begin it small. Just begin.

Tough times never last. Tough people do.
