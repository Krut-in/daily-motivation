# Monday 2026-10-19 · Discipline

> "You give yourself a pass. You know you should be further in life, but you keep letting you slide."

Monday is when the most generous person in your life shows up to give you a free pass: yourself. You're more lenient with you than anyone else has ever been. Today is the day to be the strict version of yourself you'd never tolerate from anyone else.

## The method

> "Excuses sound best to the person who is making them up."

Notice what excuse comes up first this morning, and write it down. Just the words. Tomorrow, when the same excuse arrives, you'll see it for what it is. A regular companion, not a true reason.

## The mindset

> "Order is built before urgency arrives."

Discipline is not intensity. Intensity spikes and disappears. Discipline is a quiet prepayment against the version of life where everything costs more because you waited.

## Today's call

Pick one non-negotiable action and do it in the first hour you control. Do not improve the plan first. Execute the opening rep.

Discipline is just self-respect with a schedule.
