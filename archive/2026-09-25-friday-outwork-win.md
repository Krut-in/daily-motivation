## The method

If your work is making someone uncomfortable enough to laugh, you're probably on something. The path from ridiculed to revered runs straight through the part where you keep going while being misunderstood. Today is one of those days.

## The mindset

Friday will surface the dream killers. They love you and they mean well, but they can't see past their own ceiling, so they ask you to lower yours. Don't argue. Just keep working. Your output will eventually answer the question for both of you.

## Today's call

A strong Friday changes the tone of the whole week.
