# Monday 2026-08-24 · Discipline

> "If nothing changes, nothing changes."

Monday is the day every plan you made on Sunday meets actual gravity. The version of you that wrote down the goals last night is at war with the version of you who has to act on them this morning. The Monday version wins by default unless you intervene.

## The method

> "Discipline is action taken before mood files an appeal."

The first decision should be too small to debate and too useful to skip. Put your body into motion, open the document, lace the shoes, start the timer. Momentum does not arrive first. It follows the first honest move.

## The mindset

> "Your mind will always choose the path of least resistance. Train it to choose the path of most resistance instead."

Discipline is the choice your mind makes after you've trained it to choose against itself. It feels alien at first because your default neural circuit is set to ease. Every act of friction is rewiring.

## Today's call

Pick one non-negotiable action and do it in the first hour you control. Do not improve the plan first. Execute the opening rep.

What you do daily becomes who you are eventually.
