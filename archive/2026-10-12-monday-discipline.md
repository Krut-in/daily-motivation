# Monday 2026-10-12 · Discipline

> "If nothing changes, nothing changes."

Monday is the day every plan you made on Sunday meets actual gravity. The version of you that wrote down the goals last night is at war with the version of you who has to act on them this morning. The Monday version wins by default unless you intervene.

## The method

> "Discipline is the decision to make the first move clean."

Start with the action that creates the most downstream order. Maybe that is training, planning, writing, or the one difficult task that prevents the rest of the day from pretending to be productive.

## The mindset

> "You can't just have energy when you got energy."

Energy is a result of discipline, not a prerequisite for it. The disciplined person creates energy by acting. The undisciplined person waits for energy that won't arrive without action. The order matters more than the effort.

## Today's call

Pick the one decision today that your easier self would skip. Maybe a morning workout, or 90 focused minutes before you open email. Choose it before your mood has a chance to vote.

What you do daily becomes who you are eventually.
