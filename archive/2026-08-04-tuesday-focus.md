# Tuesday 2026-08-04 · Focus

> "Focus is the courage to close doors that still look useful."

Tuesday is where good options start pretending to be obligations. A call here, a side task there, a little harmless checking. None of it looks fatal alone. Together it steals the depth the real work needs.

## The method

> "Get rid of the debate. Make the binary code decision: yes or no."

Most lost focus is just unmade decisions sitting in your head. Should I do this? Should I check that? Each unmade decision drains the battery. Convert each one into a yes or no, and the battery refills.

## The mindset

> "The next level needs less noise than the current one tolerates."

Your attention is not weak because distraction is available. It is weakened by repeated permission. Today, remove the permission and see how much stronger the work becomes.

## Today's call

Block 90 minutes for the one task that would make the day meaningful. Close every input that is not required for that task.

A focused day is a protected day.
