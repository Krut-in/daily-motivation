# Thursday 2026-09-10 · Belief

> "You don't stop when you're tired. You stop when you're done."

Thursday is when the tired version of you starts asking permission to lower the bar. The reasonable case for stopping looks airtight, and your body and mind both agree. The done version of you doesn't show up until later, but only if you keep going.

## The method

> "Confidence grows when your actions stop contradicting your claims."

Do not try to silence the critic by arguing with it all day. Out-evidence it. Keep one promise that is small, visible, and finished before noon. Let completion answer the noise.

## The mindset

> "Trust yourself by giving yourself proof, not speeches."

Self-trust is built in exact units. Not vague effort. Not big intentions. Exact promises kept enough times that your nervous system starts believing your own plans again.

## Today's call

Pick one thing today that the doubting version of you would stop short on. Push past where they'd stop. Notice how the belief shifts after, not before, the action.

Belief is built by the actions belief should have caused.
