# Thursday 2026-10-01 · Belief

> "The voice that wins is usually the voice you rehearse most."

Thursday gives the inner critic enough material to sound convincing. It will point at every miss and call it identity. That is the trick. A miss is an event. Identity is what you choose next.

## The method

> "It took me years to believe I could do it. That's the biggest thing you'll have to overcome."

Belief catches up to action, not the other way around. The Thursday version of you who acts as though the thing is possible eventually becomes the version who knows it. Don't wait for the belief. Create the evidence.

## The mindset

> "The only way out is to step forward and face your fears."

Belief isn't a feeling that arrives. It's an action you take in spite of the lack of feeling. You step forward not because you're sure, but because the only way to find out whether you're capable is to act as if you are.

## Today's call

Write down today's most active inner-critic line: the exact sentence it keeps repeating. Then write the response, one sentence of evidence that contradicts it. Read both at noon.

The stronger voice gets stronger when it has proof.
