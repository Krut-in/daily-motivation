# Monday 2026-07-27 · Discipline

> "The longer you debate the first rep, the more power the excuse collects."

Monday gives your easier self a courtroom. It wants to argue timing, sleep, energy, and whether today is really the day. Do not attend the hearing. Start before the arguments get organized.

## The method

> "Discipline is the ability to give yourself a command, and then follow it."

Make the command precise: not 'work hard today' but 'work on X for 90 minutes starting at 9.' Vague commands get vague follow-through. Specific commands get executed because there's nothing left to negotiate.

## The mindset

> "You have to learn to do when you don't want to do."

Discipline isn't about willpower. It's about removing the negotiation between you and the action. The disciplined person doesn't argue with themselves about whether to start. They just start, and the body follows.

## Today's call

Pick the one decision today that your easier self would skip. Maybe a morning workout, or 90 focused minutes before you open email. Choose it before your mood has a chance to vote.

Monday is won before the excuse gets eloquent.
